Dora Biro, a behavioural biologist and professor at the University of Rochester in New York, studies how homing pigeons learn from one another. By fitting them with GPS trackers, introducing new variables into flocks and observing how they respond, Biro has seen how pigeons’ flight routes improve in environments where they can share knowledge. In this talk from the Intelligence Everywhere festival held at Santa Monica College in October 2026, Biro discusses how these fascinating experiments were conducted, as well as what they tell us about the way in which collective intelligence can best be cultivated in human realms.
